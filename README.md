# SAHAYA — Rural Venture Testing & Decision Engine

## 🌱 Overview

**SAHAYA** is an AI-powered Rural Venture Testing & Decision Engine designed to help rural women entrepreneurs evaluate whether a business idea is viable before investing significant money.

The platform guides entrepreneurs through a structured process of **opportunity discovery, demand validation, unit economics, risk assessment, and stress testing** to support clear, evidence-based business decisions.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Rural women entrepreneurs often have promising business ideas but may face uncertainty about market demand, pricing, costs, distribution, risks, and financial viability.

SAHAYA helps address this challenge by providing a structured framework to test a business idea before making a major investment.

## 💡 Solution

SAHAYA allows users to describe their business idea and analyze it through multiple stages:

* Opportunity Discovery
* Demand Validation
* Pricing & Unit Economics
* Route-to-Market Channel Comparison
* Rural Business Risk Analysis
* Venture Twin & What-If Scenario Analysis
* Business Stress Testing
* Key Uncertainty Identification
* Smallest Practical Market Experiment
* Final Venture Decision
* 30-Day Action Plan

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🔍 Opportunity Discovery

Helps users examine their business opportunity and identify potential areas for exploration.

### 📊 Demand Validation

Provides a structured approach to assess and validate potential customer demand.

### 💰 Pricing & Unit Economics

Users can enter:

* Raw material cost per batch
* Batch size
* Packaging cost per unit
* Transport cost per delivery
* Units per delivery trip
* Selling price per unit
* Commission or markup percentage
* Expected monthly spoilage percentage

These inputs support business-level economic analysis.

### 🚚 Route-to-Market Comparison

Helps compare different sales and distribution channels to identify suitable ways to reach customers.

### ⚠️ Rural Business Risk Analysis

Identifies important risks that could affect the viability of the proposed rural venture.

### 🔄 What-If Scenario Analysis

Allows entrepreneurs to explore how changes in important assumptions could affect their business.

### 💥 Business Stress Test

Challenges the business model by testing unfavorable conditions and potential weaknesses.

### ❓ Decision Flip

Identifies the key uncertainty that could significantly change the final business decision.

### 🧪 Smallest Practical Market Experiment

Helps define a practical, small-scale experiment that can be used to test the business idea before committing significant resources.

### 🏆 Final Decision

Generates a structured **Rural Venture Decision Card** based on the analysis.

### 📅 30-Day Action Plan

Provides a practical action plan for the next 30 days to help move the validated idea toward implementation.

## 👩‍💼 Target Users

SAHAYA is primarily designed for:

* Rural women entrepreneurs

The goal is not simply to provide an answer, but to help entrepreneurs understand **why** a business idea may or may not be viable and what they should test next.
